## 5. Recommended contribution package

1. **Replication across model generations and two original benchmarks.** Establish whether the matched text control explains comparable fractions of the apparent gap, with exact model/revision/settings and clear benchmark limits.
2. **A consequential measurement audit.** Demonstrate which label errors change the scientific interpretation, using blinded human labels, population weights, separate refusal and harm, and uncertainty. Merely reporting judge kappa is insufficient.
3. **A bounded prediction result if it works.** Test the predeclared held-out architecture forecast. Negative R-squared, failed folds and lack of improvement over the mean baseline are reportable results, not reasons to swap endpoints.

The best title direction is a question about when the image matters. Avoid “first”, “universal”, “we disentangle all multimodal safety”, “vision causes misalignment”, or “no trade-offs exist”. If modern models reproduce the original residual visual effect, that is still an informative boundary condition. If measurement corrections erase apparent differences, that may be the strongest result. Do not choose the paper's conclusion before the confirmatory evidence.

## 6. Additional benchmarks: useful later, not automatically in v7

MM-SafetyBench TYPO and FigStep directly address the typographic question. XSTest is a benign control, not a third attack benchmark. If the paper makes broader claims, a separately approved semantic-vision benchmark such as VLSBench/HoliSafe can test the boundary where text transcription cannot preserve visual meaning. DUAL-Bench is relevant for visual safe-completion/over-refusal. USB offers wider coverage but adds a different evaluation scope.

Do not flatten these datasets into a single pooled ASR. Their harmfulness definitions, benign subsets and required visual context differ. Check data licenses, original IDs, judged stimuli, and the suitability of text-only guards before adding any. Current Amendment 5 authorizes only the two attack benchmarks and its benign controls; further datasets require an addendum.

**Recommended candidate question:** When can text-led testing reduce the cost of typographic VLM safety evaluation, and which harmful image responses does it miss? Novelty is unresolved. The possible contribution is a human-audited empirical limit and a validated allocation policy, not a new name for paired tests or a claim that text predicts vision for the first time.

Existing exploratory motivation is reproducible with `tools/vlm_saved_analysis.py` from `v6_full/results_v6.json` (SHA256 `41832d972afc80aa798837d1449110516703255244620cf953811972b73e767a`). For Qwen2-VL-2B, Qwen3Guard's frozen NC-adjusted E/C refusal table has 31 text-only refusals and 21 image-only refusals among 304 valid pairs: the net difference is 10/304, while 52/304 pairs change label. These are refusal labels, not harmfulness labels, and one historical completion per condition does not establish stable item propensities. Neither equivalence nor a new safety result follows from this table.

Before adoption, specify a pilot on already-seen development items: verify exact item/stimulus identities, recover paired harm labels from private caches without printing responses, and human-review a probability sample covering agreements as well as disagreements. Existing marginal harm rates cannot recover a joint harm table. Human reviewers need stimulus and response evidence, blinded model/guard identities and a fixed rubric. Separate recognition failure, refusal, harmless non-refusal and substantive harmful assistance.

If that pilot supports further work, draft an addendum with two distinct objectives: estimate total harmful-response rate; identify harmful image responses under a fixed evaluation cost. For the second objective compare all-image testing, category-stratified random image testing, and a frozen text-led allocation with a random audit of supposedly low-risk items. Count text generation, image generation, guards, preprocessing and model loading. Report missed harmful image responses and uncertainty alongside cost; exclude unresolved labels from neither accounting nor worst-case bounds. Every item needs positive, recorded audit probability if a population risk estimator is claimed. Freeze policies and acceptable error/cost criteria on development data, then test fresh items and declared architecture/backbone splits. Eight related checkpoints are not eight independent architectures. Actual image outcomes must be collected on the evaluation set to assess misses, even if the simulated policy would skip them; this validation collection does not itself save inference. Repeated completions on a prespecified subset would distinguish one-draw disagreement from reproducible changes in response propensity, with their extra cost declared. Typography remains the primary scope; semantic-image substitution would require separately validated captions/evidence and an approved benchmark addition.

The no-go conditions are consequential: if human-reviewed discordance is negligible, judge mistakes account for it, the policy cannot outperform random selection at equal measured cost, or intervals are too wide, do not headline an efficient replacement method. A negative boundary result may still be useful if adequately supported; otherwise retain a narrower replication/measurement paper. More models or benchmarks alone do not rescue weak evidence. No new endpoint, experiment, reserved outcome or approved-plan hash has been changed by this proposal.

### 11.2 Synthesis: what can actually become a paper?

**Recommended main track: a consequential measurement and replication study.** The testable contribution is whether a human-referenced, provenance-checked analysis changes the paired text/image conclusion on the original typographic benchmarks across a declared achieved panel. It must survive comparison with FigStep, MMJailBench, MSTS and ELITE. Counts, label validity, model failures and capped-output sensitivity are part of the evidence rather than inconvenient footnotes. The pilot supports engineering feasibility and an observed matched-control pattern; it cannot establish the contribution. If audited corrections are small, report that result and make replication/boundaries the claim. A top venue is not assured.

**Conditional second track: text-led evaluation with an explicit missed-harm constraint.** Separate estimating a mean harmfulness score from discovering harmful image responses. Compare image census, category-stratified random selection, and a fixed text-led rule with a random low-risk audit; include tinyBenchmarks, FAQ and robust active inference as comparators. Count all target, guard, readback, preprocessing and loading costs. Proposed loss is missed harmful image responses per scheduled item, not refusal disagreement or unvalidated judge confidence. Also show conditional recall with its denominator; it is undefined if there are no harmful image responses. A per-item paired human reference is needed. Frozen-model inference remains allowed, but fitting a new predictor would need clarification under the no-training rule; start with a prespecified rule and established descriptive baselines. An empirical frontier is a possible contribution; neither a new risk-control theorem nor guaranteed cost savings is currently established. The present pilot provides no positive missed-harm result.

**Optional boundary, outside the initial see-saw: input carrier × output protocol.** Ask whether the E/C relationship survives a fixed free-text versus structured-output instruction while words/intent and refusal affordances are controlled. Grounding safety itself is already known from the October paper. A clean study must distinguish invalid formatting, capability failure, valid refusal and harmful completion, and include benign tasks. Begin, if approved, with a supported text/JSON protocol rather than assuming grounding support on our old anchors. Verify open data and adapters; do not infer harmful actions from a text-only refusal judge. This is a separately budgeted proposal, not an automatic extension of the present pilot.

**Keep precision and evaluator-input robustness as diagnostics.** Known compression and judge-attack work supplies controls and limitations. The current nonfinite model failures do not demonstrate a compression mechanism. A trained StableJudge defense, new pruning study, audio/video benchmark, clinical benchmark, or a broad agent-safety paper would scatter this project's budget and claims. Read them to improve design; do not add all of them to the experiment.

### 11.3 Decision rules before further collection

1. Preserve completed evidence; report the pilot separately; retain the original degenerate interval flags with the reviewed warning. Do not silently retrofit better-looking results.
2. Human-review the small pilot as development material if useful, keeping the existing required reference/retest separate. Freeze grouping and label splits before scoring changes. Agreement and disagreement rows both need coverage; do not calibrate a population from a convenience audit.
3. Draft a reviewable addendum choosing the main estimand, audit design, achieved model panel, handling of cap hits and missing labels, uncertainty method, and any new benchmark/protocol. Preserve Amendment 5's historical hash.
4. For the measurement track, success means trustworthy supported/refuted/inconclusive conclusions, not a minimum dramatic correction. For the cost track, require improvement over matched-cost random selection while respecting a predeclared missed-harm bound on fresh data; otherwise do not claim replacement or efficiency.
5. Use the current [paper draft](../paper/manuscript.md) as a traceable exploratory manuscript. Human-corrected estimates, fresh replication, and a genuine efficiency frontier remain explicitly unmeasured. Do not add new experiments simply to rescue the original hypothesis.

### 14.3 What our archived annotations actually demonstrate

Reproducible sources: `tools/analyze_comprehension_selection.py`; aggregate `reports/comprehension_selection_2026-10-20.json`; figure with the same basename. Private inputs are hash-validated original package and separate A/B exports. No prompts, replies or identifiers appear in outputs. This is a **post-hoc demonstration on previously seen development data**, not an amendment of the running study.

| Original assisted Reviewer A | P_txt | P_img | S_txt | S_img | Plain image−text | Steps image−text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All eight original items | 4/8 | 5/8 | 5/8 | 8/8 | +12.5pp | +37.5pp |
| Naively keep UNDERSTANDING separately in each cell | 4/8 | 0/3 | 3/6 | 3/3 | −50pp | +50pp |
| Retain identical UNDERSTANDING items across all four cells | 0/3 | 0/3 | 0/3 | 3/3 | 0pp | +100pp |

The middle row appears to meet the sign/margin pattern but compares **different item populations**, so it is not a paired crossover. It removes generic-refusal answers with uncertain comprehension while retaining other answers. With correct common-item matching the strict negative plain effect disappears. The three retained items do not estimate a full-cohort or latent-comprehension effect. B reproduces the calculation because its refusal/comprehension labels match A's; this is not independent human replication. The original 48-answer set has 17 uncertain comprehension labels, all attached to refusals; uncertainty is not evidence of actual failure to understand.

This counterexample concerns **our explicitly constructed naive filter**. It does not show that MMJailBench excludes generic refusals, that its reported CASR is wrong, or that all comprehension conditioning is invalid. Its B.3 mismatch definition is different. Known selection/conditioning problems and EviSafe's caveat substantially limit novelty. The useful empirical question is how often a carefully specified version changes real multimodal conclusions under independent review.

### 14.4 Ranked claim candidates and stopping decisions

| Priority | Falsifiable candidate | Closest coverage / novelty verdict | What would support, refute or leave unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A response-evidence comprehension filter can manufacture an apparent within-checkpoint carrier crossover through differential item retention | Concrete project counterexample above; **established statistical issue, potentially useful domain-specific empirical audit; novelty unestablished**. Compare MMJailBench §4.2/B.3 and EviSafe B.7 | Support beyond this case requires declared filters, all denominator/pair funnels and untouched human labels across more item sets. A pattern surviving common cohorts may be behavioral; absence of practical changes limits value. No current causal mechanism claim |
| 2 | Increasing the visible response window changes a carrier conclusion through differential evaluator error rather than a corresponding human-label change | **Known general length/judge sensitivity**, exact selected factorial consequence unresolved; Beyer §4/A.6, StrongREJECT, JADES and Uncontrolled Variable S29 | Existing accepted window run supplies outputs. Use fixed human rubric and exact common pairs. Human/guard contrast changes need separating. Equal conclusions are limited robustness; caps/missingness can leave it unresolved. Do not alter the current primary endpoint |
| 3 | A wrapper/carrier's higher harmful-request refusal mainly reflects increased benign-request refusal, not better discrimination | **Partly covered closely** by VSCBench, VLSU §4.4, SafetyPairs ROC and October grounding controls | A future matched benign/harmful × wrapper × carrier study needs independently reviewed intent, harmful assistance and utility. Same raw changes alone are insufficient; a benefit after matched benign costs weakens the claim. New scope/budget needed |
| Defer | Text-only testing can replace image safety tests | MSTS/VLSU/CSR-Bench and earlier project plan; **not supported by matched means** | Needs held-out paired missed-harm criteria and enough harmful events; current tiny pilot and model panel do not support substitution |
| Reject as standalone | First human/LLM disagreement, new comprehension-adjusted metric, basic image-presence effect, structured-output safety gap | **Known/closely covered** | Adding checkpoints or renaming existing controls does not repair the overlap |

**Recommendation:** develop one coherent measurement paper around whether the *same paired conclusion* survives representation, denominator, response-window and reference-label choices. The denominator example is the clearest new result from our own saved evidence, but too small and assisted to carry a general paper. Let the active fixed study finish, preserve its outputs, perform the planned blind review, and decide whether there is enough consequential evidence to justify a fresh, separately approved validation. If not, report a narrow replication/negative-result study rather than manufacture a breakthrough.
